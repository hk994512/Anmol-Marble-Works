Our Story

At ANMOL Marble & Tile Works, we believe that your space is a reflection of your personality. Based in Islamabad and Rawalpindi, we have spent over a decade perfecting the art of marble fitting, tile installation, and complete construction finishing.

Under the leadership of Kaleem Ahmad, our team of 50+ skilled workers has completed 500+ projects across residential, commercial, and industrial sectors.
