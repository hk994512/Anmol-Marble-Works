At ANMOL Marble & Tile Works, we believe that flooring and stone surfaces are the very soul of a luxury home. Based in Islamabad and Rawalpindi, our craft spans over a decade of hands-on experience on more than 500 prestigious private villas, commercial plazas, and embassy residences.

Under the leadership of Kaleem Ahmad, our crews operate with modern rotary diamond polishers, precision tile saws, and digital leveling devices to eliminate uneven joints, hollow sounds, and dull surfaces.
